Habits that work

Practise 20 to 30 minutes daily rather than one long session a week. Play new things slowly with the metronome and raise the tempo by 5 BPM only when it is clean. Record yourself once a week and listen back. Tune before every session, and keep a capo and a clip-on tuner in the case.
