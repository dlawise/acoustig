5-minute warm-up

＋
  1. Breathe in for 4, out on a steady 'sss' for 10.
  2. Lip trills, sliding gently up and down.
  3. Hum a five-note scale, up a semitone each time.
  4. Sing 'ah' on the same scale, then back down.
  5. Sing a verse of a song quietly.

🎚 Tuner

–

Tap Start, then pluck one string.

New nylon strings

＋
  1. Nylon stretches a lot at first. Tune up slowly in small steps, then retune several times over a few days.
  2. Thin high strings snap when pushed too high. Tune up to the note from below and stop at the note. Never crank past it.
  3. If a string feels too tight, choose a lower tuning above, for example a whole step down. Everything in the app follows it.
  4. Your own tuning lets you set each string to whatever note you actually have.

Tips for an acoustic

＋
  1. Sit in a quiet room and hold the phone 20 to 30 cm from the soundhole.
  2. Pluck one string at medium strength and let it ring. The reading settles after about a second.
  3. Tune up to the note: if you go sharp, loosen below the note and tighten back up.
  4. Tune E, A, D, G, B, e, then go round again, because tension changes as you go.
  5. If the reading jumps, tap the note to hear it and lock to that string.
  6. Take the capo off first.
  7. Nylon strings are quieter and stretch for the first week or two, so hold the phone closer (10 to 20 cm) and re-check the tuning often.
